SPACE BLAST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Space Blast ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile game developed and published as a personal project. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By installing or using Space Blast, you agree to the terms in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information we collect automatically (via third-party SDKs):

  • Advertising identifier (GAID / IDFA): A randomized device identifier used by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) to deliver and measure ads. You can reset it at any time from your device's privacy settings.
  • Approximate device info: Device model, OS version, country (from IP), and locale — used to pick ads relevant to your region.
  • Ad interaction events: Whether you watched an ad to completion, closed it early, or installed an advertised app. Used to bill advertisers and detect fraud.
  • Approximate location (region only): Your advertising partner may estimate your general region from your device's IP address in order to serve relevant ads. We do not request or access precise location, and the app holds no location permissions.
  • App performance diagnostics: App launch time, hang rate, and energy usage, collected by Google to measure SDK reliability and detect fraud.

Information stored locally on your device:

  • High score: Your personal best score is stored in your device's local preferences. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it.
  • Settings: Music on/off, vibration on/off, and sound effects volume.

Information we do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any contact info
  • Your precise location — the app does not request the location permission
  • Your photos, contacts, calendar, or messages
  • Your health and fitness data, or financial data beyond ad spending
  • Any in-app payment data (the game is free, no purchases)

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • Show you ads (free game, ads fund the development)
  • Measure how many people play Space Blast and for how long
  • Detect and prevent fraud (e.g. automated ad clicks)
  • Remember your game settings between sessions

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising outside of Space Blast.

4. Third-Party Services

Space Blast uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — serves ads. Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services (Android) — required by AdMob to fetch and display ads.

Google uses the advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. You can learn more in Google's advertising policies, the partner sites privacy page, and the Privacy Tools hub.

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to read them.

Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. You can delete or reset your advertising identifier at any time from your device's privacy or ad settings.

4a. EEA, UK and Other Regional Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, Switzerland, or another region with similar data protection law, you have additional rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR:

  • Lawful basis: we rely on consent for personalised advertising, and on legitimate interests for fraud prevention and basic, aggregated measurement.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw consent at any time through the Google Ads settings linked in Section 6, and your choice applies going forward.
  • Access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection: contact us at the email in Section 11 and we will respond within 30 days.
  • Complaints: you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

We do not transfer your data to third countries for our own purposes. Google's own processing is described in its privacy policy.

5. Children

Space Blast is intended for users aged 13 and over, which is the target audience declared in our Google Play listing. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

The app declares to Google AdMob that it is not child-directed, but it is rated for a general audience (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone) and may be used by teenagers. Google treats users aged 13-15 as potentially under the age of digital consent in some countries, so we tag our ad requests for teen treatment: this restricts ad serving to age-appropriate, non-personalised inventory. We also restrict all served ads to a G rating, which is safe for all ages. Where our ad partner identifies a user as under-age, they apply child-directed treatment in line with COPPA and similar laws.

Because the game is free and contains no purchases, no purchases or payments are made by children or collected on their behalf. Users under 13 must not install the app without a parent or guardian's permission.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • Reset your advertising ID at any time — Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking. You can also do it here: myadcenter.google.com (Google Ad Center lets you review and change what Google knows about you).
  • Opt out of personalized ads — turn off ad personalization in the same Google Ads settings, or manage it via adssettings.google.com. Note: opting out does not remove ads, it only makes them less targeted.
  • Manage your data — review activity across Google services at myactivity.google.com, and see how Google uses data from sites and apps in our partner sites privacy page.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data (high score, settings).
  • Contact us at the email below with any privacy questions or to request data deletion.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored data (high score, settings) stays on your device until you uninstall the app. AdMob retains its data per Google's retention policy.

8. International Transfers

AdMob may process your advertising identifier on servers in the United States and other countries. By using Space Blast, you understand your data may be transferred outside your home country.

9. Security

We rely on Google AdMob's industry-standard security to protect advertising data. Locally stored data is protected by your device's normal security (lock screen, encryption).

No method of transmission over the internet, however, is 100% secure.
